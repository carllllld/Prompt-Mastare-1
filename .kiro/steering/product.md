# Product Overview

OptiPrompt is an AI-powered SaaS platform for generating and optimizing Swedish real estate listing texts (mäklartexter). The system uses OpenAI GPT-5.2 with reasoning capabilities to produce high-quality, broker-realistic property descriptions.

## Core Features

- AI-driven text generation with multi-stage pipeline (7 steps)
- Multiple subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Premium) with usage quotas
- Team collaboration with shared styles and invitations
- Personal writing style profiles
- Stripe payment integration
- Email verification and password reset flows
- WebSocket real-time updates
- PDF export functionality

## Target Users

Swedish real estate brokers and agencies who need professional, compliant property listing texts that avoid AI clichés while maintaining natural broker language.

## Quality Focus

The system emphasizes broker realism over generic AI output, with extensive validation rules, forbidden phrase detection, and multi-stage quality gates to ensure texts sound authentically human and professionally written.
